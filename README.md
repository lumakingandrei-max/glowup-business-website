## Run locally
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the `glowup_business_website` folder.
3. Double-click `index.html` or open it in a browser.
4. An internet connection is needed for Bootstrap, icons, Google Fonts, and the remote photos.

## Publish free with GitHub Pages
1. Sign in to GitHub and create a new **public** repository, for example `glowup-business-website`.
2. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` to the repository's root folder. You may also upload this README.
3. Open the repository's **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Choose branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then save.
6. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish. Your URL will look like:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/glowup-business-website/`
7. Open the URL on a phone and computer to test the responsive layout.

## Before submitting
- Confirm that the instructor accepts the proposed business and the sample financial estimates.
- Replace the proposed location and social media placeholder with real project details if required.
- Check every link and test the contact form. The form opens the visitor's email app; it does not store or send messages through a server.
- The photos are loaded from Unsplash URLs, so an internet connection is required for them to display.
- Update prices, startup costs, and monthly projections if your business plan uses different estimates.

## Financial estimate note
All prices and financial figures are illustrative Philippine-peso estimates for a classroom proposal. They are not verified supplier quotations or a promise of actual business income. The projected monthly sales use 5 appointments per day × 24 days × ₱450 average sale = ₱54,000. Estimated monthly operating expenses are ₱35,000, giving a sample operating surplus of ₱19,000 before tax and other unlisted costs.
